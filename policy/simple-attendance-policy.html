  1. 100% On-Device Offline Storage: Simple Attendance does not require user accounts and does not operate any backend servers to collect or store attendance records or biometric data.   2. No Raw Face Enrollment Photos Stored: During face registration, the application converts five head angles into mathematical feature vectors directly in temporary memory (RAM) and immediately discards the raw camera frames. Facial data is never transmitted to our servers.
  3. Clear Allocation of Legal Responsibility: We provide Simple Attendance solely as an independent, on-device software tool. The Store Owner / Employer is the sole Data Controller and Data Processor, bearing 100% of the legal responsibility for notifying employees, obtaining valid and lawful employee consent before registering facial data or capturing verification photos, providing alternative attendance methods (6-digit Attendance Codes), and safeguarding the physical device as well as any exported backup (.eab) or Excel report (.xlsx) files.

2. Categories of Data Processed and Stored Locally on the Device

All business and operational data categories listed below are created, processed, and stored exclusively inside the isolated internal storage of the Store Owner's device:

2.1. Store Data

  • Data Processed: Store Name, optional Store Photo (resized and saved locally inside the application's private storage), and an internal employee numbering sequence.
  • Purpose: Displaying store identity on the administration dashboard, the Kiosk attendance screen, and monthly Excel timesheet headers.

2.2. Employee Data

  • Data Processed:
    • Employee Full Name;
    • Optional Job Title / Role;
    • Optional Employee Profile Avatar (saved locally inside the application's private storage — used strictly for visual profile display, completely separate from and never used for facial recognition; if omitted, an initials avatar is displayed);
    • Auto-generated, permanent Employee ID (such as 0001, 0002… — never reused or reassigned even after an employee is archived);
    • Employment Status (Active or Archived) and scheduled status effective dates;
    • Enabled/disabled attendance methods and a unique 6-digit Attendance Code (historical codes are tracked locally to ensure no 6-digit code is ever duplicated or reused across the store);
    • Weekly shift assignments.
  • Purpose: Managing staff rosters, scheduling shifts, authenticating employees at the attendance kiosk, and calculating monthly timesheets.

2.3. Face Recognition & Biometric Data

  • What Is Extracted and Stored:
    • When the Store Owner registers an employee's face (after explicitly confirming the mandatory in-app face data consent notice), the application uses the front camera to scan five head angles (Front, Left, Right, Up, Down).
    • On-device machine learning models convert these five poses into mathematical feature vectors stored exclusively in the local database on the device.
  • Strict Biometric Privacy Safeguards:
    • No Raw Face Enrollment Images Saved: Camera frames captured during face registration, liveness verification, and live attendance scanning exist only momentarily in volatile memory (RAM) for a fraction of a second to extract mathematical feature vectors and are immediately discarded. Raw enrollment frames are never saved as image files.
    • Irreversible Mathematical Representation: The stored feature vectors are one-way numerical representations used solely to compute similarity scores; they are not image files and cannot be reverse-engineered to reconstruct a photograph of the employee's face.
    • 100% On-Device Processing: Face detection, anti-spoofing liveness checks (such as detecting screen replay or prompting the user to blink or turn their head), and facial matching execute 100% offline on the device.
    • Zero Server Transmission: Facial feature vectors are never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party for attendance processing, advertising, or AI training.

2.4. Attendance, Schedule & Approval Data

  • Data Processed:
    • Actual check-in and check-out timestamps recorded in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC);
    • Attendance method used (Face or Attendance Code) and facial similarity percentage;
    • Store schedule version history, shift definitions, calculation rules (daily or shift-based calculation), and valid punch window limits;
    • Exception approvals recorded by the Store Owner (Forgot check-in, Forgot check-out, Late in, Early out, Annual leave, Sick leave, Business trip, Other, Rejected) along with explanatory notes.
  • Timestamp Immutability: Raw attendance timestamps are immutable once recorded. The application does not provide manual check-in/out time editing or proxy punching, ensuring transparent and tamper-evident records for both Store Owners and Employees.

2.5. Verification Photos

  • On Successful Attendance: The application captures and saves a compressed verification photo only if the Store Owner explicitly enables "Save verification photos" under Settings → Attendance. When this setting is turned off, successful punches do not save photos.
  • On Security Alert Events: Whenever the front camera is available, the application automatically attempts to capture a verification photo when an invalid Attendance Code is entered or when a suspected face spoofing attempt is detected, providing internal audit evidence for the Store Owner.
  • Storage Location: Stored exclusively inside the application's private internal storage, hidden from the device's public photo gallery, and never embedded into exported Excel (.xlsx) reports.

2.6. Internal Security Alert Data

  • Data Processed:
    • Invalid Code Attempts: Timestamp, the invalid 6-digit code entered, read/unread status, and the associated verification photo (if captured);
    • Suspected Face Spoof Alerts: Timestamp, spoofing reason detected (such as screen replay from another device, a secondary face detected in the frame, or abnormal screen reflection), matched employee candidate (if any), and alert photo;
    • Suspicious Match Alerts: Flagged on attendance records when an employee selects a candidate with a lower similarity score than the top match or when multiple employees tie for the highest similarity score.
  • Purpose: Enabling the Store Owner to detect unauthorized code guessing, proxy punching, or screen-replay spoofing at the kiosk.

3. Device Permissions & Disclosures

Simple Attendance requests only the minimum permissions necessary for its features:

  1. Camera Permission:
    • Just-in-Time Request & Prominent Disclosure: Requested only when you open a camera-dependent feature and always preceded by a clear in-app disclosure explaining how camera frames and facial data are processed locally.

    • Foreground-Only Execution: The camera operates only while the corresponding screen is actively open in front of the user; the application never records video or captures images covertly in the background or when the screen is off.
    • Non-Camera Alternative: Camera hardware and permission are optional. If Camera permission is denied, the store can operate completely using 6-Digit Attendance Codes.
  2. System Biometric / Device Lock Authentication:
    • Used strictly via the standard Android system authentication prompt to verify the Store Owner's identity when unlocking the admin dashboard (App Lock), stopping Kiosk Attendance Mode (Stop attendance), restoring a backup, or deleting all app data. The application only receives a pass/fail result from the operating system and never accesses your system fingerprint or face unlock credentials.
  3. Network Access:
    • Never used to transmit attendance or employee data. Used strictly for: (i) Verifying accurate UTC time against time-tampering; (ii) Loading this Privacy Policy page; and (iii) Managing Premium subscriptions/unlocks via Google Play Billing / RevenueCat and serving Google AdMob advertisements in the free version.

    • The application does not request broad access to your device's shared storage. Exporting .eab backups, exporting .xlsx reports, or selecting profile images is handled safely through the standard Android system file picker and share sheet.

4. Data Storage Location & Security Safeguards

  1. Isolated Internal Storage & Device Encryption:
    • All databases, configuration settings, and photo directories reside inside the private application storage on the device, protected from other applications by Android operating system isolation and device file-based encryption.
  2. Automatic Cloud Backup Disabled:
    • Automatic operating-system cloud backup and device-to-device data transfer are disabled for Simple Attendance. Biometric feature vectors and attendance logs are never silently backed up to cloud drives or transferred to another device without the Store Owner's explicit action.
  3. Admin App Lock & 60-Second Auto-Relock:
    • Store Owners can enable App Lock (Settings → Security) using either All (biometrics or screen lock) or Biometrics only. Whenever the application remains in the background for 60 seconds or more, administrative access automatically locks.
  4. Kiosk Screen Pinning & Safe Exit Protection:
    • Attendance Mode supports Android Screen Pinning to prevent unauthorized users from exiting the kiosk to alter the system clock or uninstall the software. Stopping Attendance Mode always requires Store Owner authentication before unpinning the screen.

5. Encrypted Backups (.eab), Excel Exports (.xlsx) & User Custody

5.1. .eab Backup Encryption

  • When the Store Owner creates a backup (Settings → Backup & data → Create backup), the archive is encrypted end-to-end using AES-256-GCM with a cryptographic key derived via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (210,000 iterations) from a password chosen by the Store Owner (minimum 8 characters).
  • Optional Data Controls During Backup:
    • Include face data: The Store Owner explicitly chooses whether to include facial feature vectors in the .eab backup file. If unchecked, facial vectors are excluded from the backup archive, and restored employees will simply be marked as needing face re-registration.
    • Include attendance & alert photos: The Store Owner explicitly chooses whether to bundle verification and alert photos into the .eab archive.
    • Device-specific security settings (such as the current App Lock state on the phone) are not included in .eab archives.

5.2. Monthly Excel Timesheet Exports (.xlsx)

  • Exported .xlsx files contain only tabular attendance summaries and daily punch logs (Employee Name, Employee ID, Shifts, In/Out timestamps, Approvals, Work Credits, Worked Hours, Late/Early durations). Facial feature vectors and verification photos are never included in .xlsx files.

5.3. Zero-Knowledge Password & User Responsibility for Exported Files

  • Zero-Knowledge Encryption: We never store, transmit, or know your backup password and cannot recover passwords or decrypt .eab files under any circumstances.
  • Once an .eab backup or .xlsx report is saved outside the application or shared via third-party apps (such as Email, messaging apps, or cloud storage), that file is outside the application's control and deletion scope. The Store Owner assumes full responsibility for safeguarding exported files and passwords.

6. Data Retention & How to Delete Data

6.1. Retention Policies

  • Core Operational Data (Store profile, Employees, Schedules, Attendance timestamps, Approvals, and facial feature vectors): Retained locally on the device until the Store Owner deletes specific items, erases all app data, overwrites the store via backup restoration, or uninstalls the application.
  • Alert Data (invalid code attempts, suspected face spoof attempts, and alert flags/photos): Automatically cleaned up in the background according to the retention policy configured by the Store Owner under Settings → Backup & data → Data management:
    • Auto-delete by age: 30 days / 60 days / 90 days / Off;
    • Storage cap: 1 GB / 2 GB / 3 GB / Off (oldest alert data is deleted first until both age and storage cap limits are satisfied).
    • Note: Automated alert cleanup never deletes recorded attendance timestamps, timesheets, approvals, facial feature vectors, or recorded attendance photos.

6.2. How to Delete Data Directly Inside the Application

Because Simple Attendance does not create user accounts on a server, there is no cloud account deletion request flow; instead, the Store Owner has immediate, self-service deletion controls on the device:

  1. Delete Face Data for a Single Employee:
    • Go to Employees → [Select Employee] → Edit → Delete face data → Save. That employee's facial feature vectors are permanently erased from the local database.
  2. Delete All Alert Data:
    • Go to Settings → Backup & data → Data management → Delete all alert data (or delete individual alerts in the Alerts screen).
  3. Delete Old Attendance Photos or Archived Employee Photos:
    • Go to Settings → Backup & data → Data management:
      • Under Attendance photo cleanup, select Older than 1 month / 3 months / 6 months / 1 year and tap Delete old attendance photos; or
      • Under Archived employee photos, tap Delete archived employee photos to permanently remove profile avatars and past attendance photos of archived employees while keeping their attendance history and timesheets intact.
  4. Delete All App Data:
    • Stop Attendance Mode (if active), then navigate to Settings → Backup & data → Data management → Delete all app data.
    • After verifying your device lock and entering the confirmation prompt, 100% of the local database (Store, Employees, resetting Employee ID numbering back to 0001, Face data, Schedules, Attendance records, Approvals, Alerts), all photo files, and all settings are permanently erased from the device.
