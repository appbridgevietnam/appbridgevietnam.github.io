Privacy Policy

Expiry Reminder – Tracker

Thank you for using Expiry Reminder – Tracker. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

Expiry Reminder – Tracker is designed to work primarily offline. We do not require you to create an account or sign in.

We do not collect, store, or share personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location.

The App stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Items and their expiration dates.
  • Categories and reminder settings.
  • Notes and storage locations.
  • History of completed or expired items.
  • Photos you choose to attach to items (optional).

This information remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

2. How We Use Information

The information stored on your device is used only to provide the App's core functionality, including:

  • Managing expiration dates.
  • Displaying your saved items.
  • Scheduling reminder notifications.
  • Organizing categories and history.

We do not track your activity or use your information for profiling.

3. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not share your personal information with third parties.
  • No reminder data, notes, photos, or items are uploaded to any external server.

4. Permissions

The App may request certain Android permissions to function correctly, including:

  • Notifications – to remind you before items expire.
  • Photos and Media (optional) – only if you choose to attach images to your items.

These permissions are used solely for providing the App's intended functionality.

5. Advertising

The free version of the App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google may collect certain device identifiers and advertising data in accordance with its own Privacy Policy.

Learn more: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Data Storage

All reminder data, notes, categories, history, settings, and optional photos remain stored locally on your device.

Expiry Reminder – Tracker does not provide cloud storage, synchronization, or online backup.

If you uninstall the App or clear its data through your device settings, your saved information may be permanently deleted.

7. Security

Since the App does not transmit or store your personal information on external servers, your data remains under your control on your device.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
